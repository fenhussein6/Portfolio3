Who I Am

Dr. Fentaw Hussen Abegaz is a dedicated biochemist and instructor at Wollo University School of Veterinary Medicine, where he has been serving since September 2024. He is actively engaged in teaching, curriculum development, and research, contributing to the advancement of veterinary biochemistry and physiology education.

His academic work emphasizes outcome-based learning and instructional design, aligning with modern educational standards. Prior to joining Wollo University, Dr. Fentaw served as a lecturer at Samar University, where he built a strong foundation in higher education teaching and student mentorship.

His mission is to bridge foundational biochemical sciences with practical veterinary applications, preparing the next generation of veterinary professionals with the knowledge and critical thinking skills they need to succeed.
